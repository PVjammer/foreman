## start
- Write down the question and what answer would change a decision.

## work
- Prototype quickly in your worktree; quality doesn't matter, the answer does. Stop at the time box and report what you learned, even if it's incomplete.

## closeout
- Docs: record the question, the answer, the evidence, and the recommendation (a decision log or ADR is a good home).
- Branches: say the branch can be deleted unless something is worth keeping (then name it).
